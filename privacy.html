Privacy Policy

Spin it respects your privacy.

The app is designed to help you make quick decisions with a simple spinning wheel. We do not require an account and we do not directly collect, store, sell, or share your personal information.

Effective date: 1 January 2026

Data we collect

We do not directly collect personal data through Spin it.

This means:

  • No account registration is required.
  • No personal information is collected by us, such as your name, photos, or precise location.
  • No usage analytics are used by us.
  • No crash reporting services are used by us.
  • No cookies or similar tracking technologies are used by us inside the app.

Local processing

Your wheel options and decisions are processed on your device. The core app functionality does not require sending your wheel content to our servers.

Third-party advertising: Google AdMob

Spin it uses Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service provided by Google, to show advertisements inside the app.

Google AdMob may collect or use information such as:

  • Device information.
  • Advertising identifiers, such as the device advertising ID.
  • App usage and interaction data related to ads.
  • Approximate location.
  • Information used to deliver, measure, and improve ads.

Google may use this information to show personalized or non-personalized ads, depending on your device settings, consent choices, location, and applicable laws.

You can learn more about how Google uses data here:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Advertising Technologies

Consent for users in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland

For users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, Google requires certain disclosures and, where legally required, user consent for cookies or similar local storage and for the collection, sharing, and use of personal data for ads personalization.

Where required, the app may show a consent message allowing you to manage your advertising choices.

Children’s privacy

Spin it is not intended for children under the age of 5, or the minimum age required in your country.

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. However, because the app uses Google AdMob, Google’s advertising services may process certain device or advertising data.

The app complies with applicable child privacy laws, including COPPA where relevant.

Data sharing

We do not store user personal data on our own servers.

Any data collected by Google AdMob is handled by Google according to Google’s own privacy practices and retention policies.

Data retention

We do not sell, trade, or transfer personal information to third parties.

Because the app uses Google AdMob, certain information may be collected and processed by Google as described in Google’s own privacy policy and advertising documentation.

Your privacy choices

You can control or limit advertising tracking through your device settings.

On iOS, you can manage app tracking permissions in:

Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking

You can also reset or limit advertising identifiers through your device privacy settings where available.

Google also provides tools to manage advertising preferences through your Google account and privacy settings.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact the developer via the App Store listing.
